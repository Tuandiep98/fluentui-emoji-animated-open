Create ONE complete recognizable subject: one compact isolated winding blue river ribbon flowing between two narrow connected green banks, S-shaped path through a small cutaway terrain patch, water continues to both edges of the patch, no mountains, trees, bridges, people, sky or horizon. Soft sculpted 3D forms, clean friendly proportions, tactile but restrained material detail, warm soft studio light from upper left. Clear instantly recognizable silhouette at 64px. Full subject centered in a square with generous 12% transparent margin on every side. Genuine alpha transparency. No cast ground shadow, backdrop, floor, scenery, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, hands or extra objects beyond those explicitly listed for identifying the subject. One fully assembled final illustration; not separate parts, not a rig or a sprite sheet. All joins and orientation must already be correct in this immutable rest pose. Clean physical silhouette only. No colored halo, glow, scattered alpha specks, mist or background.
