## Runtime dependencies

The generation toolkit uses `sharp` (Apache-2.0). Its installed native packages include libvips and related image codecs with their own notices. Refer to the installed package licenses before redistributing dependency binaries. FFmpeg and libavif are external command-line tools installed separately; they are not bundled in this source repository.

The showcase has no third-party JavaScript, CSS, font, analytics, or remote asset dependency.
